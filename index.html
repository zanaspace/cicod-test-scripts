⚖️ Document Sharing Capability: 1Government vs. CICOD Drive (cDrive)

How cDrive transforms sovereign bureaucratic custody into a frictionless, zero-trust B2B enterprise collaboration engine.

Capability Dimension 1Government Drive (cde) CICOD Drive (cDrive) Enterprise Advantage
External Collaboration UX Hidden behind a checkbox ("Other MDA & Service Provider?"). Dedicated B2B Partner / Client Extranet tab with tenant directory. Zero confusion when collaborating with auditors, vendors, or external legal counsel.
Role & Permission Tiers Binary: Editor vs. View Only (Editor hard-disabled for TS/S files). 4-Tier Granular RBAC: Viewer (Watermarked), Reviewer (Markup), Editor, Co-Owner. Enables external legal & audit teams to review/comment without altering original text.
Inbound Quarantine Gate Bureaucratic registry queue (findFilesPendingAcceptance). Repurposed as Inbound Reception Desk for client deliverables. Prevents uninspected external files from polluting team drives; formal verify-and-accept step.
Compliance & Audit Logging Requires manual 5-line justification essay (#link-sharing-reason). Audit Purpose Presets (PBC Audit, M&A VDR, Customs, Contract Review). Eliminates user friction while providing structured, machine-searchable SOC 2 audit logs.
Link Expiration Controls Hardcoded 5-day expiration on authenticated links; non-configurable. Configurable Expiry: 24h (M&A), 7d, 30d (Client Audit), or Permanent. Accommodates short-lived M&A bids and long-term client engagements with instant kill-switch.
Link Security Governance Only authenticated 1Gov login or unauthenticated public URL. SSO Domain Fencing (@pwc.com only), Password-Protected, or Watermarked. Eliminates link forwarding leaks and secures external contractor delivery.
